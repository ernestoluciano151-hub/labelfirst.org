Why this site exists

Search for almost any supplement and you will find dozens of pages describing a dramatic personal transformation over 30, 60 or 90 days, often on hijacked domains, often with stock photographs attached to invented names. Some of them do not even describe the right product.

That is not a review. It is a sales page wearing a review's clothes, and it is why people have stopped trusting the format.

We publish what can be checked and we are explicit about what cannot. If a product has no published trial, we say so. If a claim has no verifiable source, we give it no weight. If we have not used something, we do not pretend we have. Our method is written down, and you can hold us to it.
