Privacy policy

LabelFirstUpdated October 2026

The short version

We do not ask you for personal information, we do not run accounts, and we do not sell data. We do use analytics to understand which articles people read, and our outbound affiliate links pass tracking information to the seller so the sale can be attributed to us.

What we collect

Analytics. We use a web analytics service to count visits and see which pages are read. This records technical information such as your approximate location by country, your browser and device type, the page you arrived from and the pages you view. We do not use it to identify individuals.

Advertising parameters. If you arrived here from an advertisement, the link you clicked may carry parameters identifying the campaign and creative. We pass these through to the seller when you click an affiliate link, so that a purchase can be matched to the advertisement that led to it. These parameters describe the advertisement, not you.

What we do not collect. We do not ask for your name, email address, postal address or payment details. There are no forms, no accounts and no newsletter on this site.

Cookies

Our analytics may set a cookie to avoid counting the same visitor twice. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the site works without them.

When you leave this site

Clicking an affiliate link takes you to a seller's website or to a retail network such as ClickBank. Once you leave, this policy no longer applies. Anything you enter there — name, address, payment details — is collected by them under their own privacy policy and terms, which you should read. We never see your payment information at any point.

Children

This site is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data held about you. Because we do not hold identifiable personal data, there is usually nothing to retrieve — but if you want to ask, contact us.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page.
