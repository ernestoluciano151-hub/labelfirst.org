About LabelFirst

A small independent site reviewing supplements and sleep products for readers in the United States.

Who publishes this

LabelFirst is published by Ernesto Luciano, who writes and edits everything here. There is no team and no newsroom. It is one person reading labels carefully and writing down what is there.

We think that is worth saying plainly, because a great deal of health-product content online is published anonymously or under invented bylines. If you want to ask who wrote something or why we reached a conclusion, there is a real person to ask.

Why it exists

Search any supplement and the results fill with pages describing a dramatic personal transformation, usually by someone who never opened the box, frequently on domains that were hijacked for the purpose. The format has been so thoroughly abused that "review" has stopped meaning anything in this category.

We are trying to make it mean something again, by a simple method: publish what can be checked, be explicit about what cannot, and never pretend to experience we have not had. The method is written down.

What this is not

  • Not medical advice. We are not clinicians. Nothing here replaces a conversation with your dentist, doctor or pharmacist.
  • Not a manufacturer. We do not make, sell or ship anything. Purchases happen on the seller's own site under their terms.
  • Not independent of commerce. We earn affiliate commissions. We would rather say that on every page than pretend to a purity we do not have.
